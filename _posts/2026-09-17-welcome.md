This is where we share our progress, what we find and our research as we prepare for Claude Certification.

## How to post
1. Click **Write a post** at the top of the page.
2. Fill in the form and click **Open in GitHub**, then **Commit changes**.
3. Your post goes live in about a minute.

Use **Progress** for weekly updates, **Findings** for things you figured out and **Research** for longer write-ups.
